# Intent: the menu as a card-sized layer, not a full-screen overlay

Closes #50.

## Problem

The menu (`Menu.qml:90-122`) is a `PanelWindow` anchored to all four edges
of the monitor. It is an `Overlay` layer with a scrim
(`Commons.Color.menu.scrim`) and exclusive keyboard focus.

The full-screen surface does two jobs:

- **It catches a click outside the card.** A `MouseArea` over the whole
  layer closes the menu.
- **It places the card.** The card is centred, and a third of the way down
  (`y` from `panel.height`). Its size is capped at `panel.width * 0.9` and
  `panel.height * 0.85`.

What that costs while the menu is open:

- **It dims the whole monitor.** That includes anything being shared in a
  call, which is the case the owner raised on 2026-11-16.
- **It covers the whole monitor.** Clicks anywhere on it belong to the menu,
  although the menu needs only a card's worth of space.

The layer exists only while the menu is open (`visible: root.opened`). This
is about what it does while open.

## Proposed outcome

- **The menu takes only its card's space** on the monitor it opens on, in
  the same place as today.
- **The rest of the monitor is not dimmed**, and stays visible as it is.
- **A click outside the card closes the menu.** That click does nothing
  else: it never reaches the app underneath.
- **The card keeps today's size rules.** It takes the same share of the
  monitor, derived from the screen and the desktop text size, and it still
  works when no focused screen can be found.
- **Nothing else changes:**
  - the keys and look;
  - exclusive keyboard focus while open;
  - the `nixarchy-microvm-menu` namespace;
  - IPC (`toggle`, `{"create":true}`);
  - keep-loaded behaviour.
- **Documentation matches.**
  - The README and `docs/usage.md` describe the menu without the dimming.
  - AGENTS.md's "full-screen menu" wording (`AGENTS.md:15`, `:45`) and the
    comments in `MicrovmView.qml` (`:11`, `:71`) say what it is now.

## Affected users and systems

- Everyone who opens the menu from a key or the Omarchy menu row.
- The bar popup (`Panel.qml`) is the shell's `KeyboardPanel`, not this
  layer, so it is unaffected.
- **Files:**
  - `Menu.qml`;
  - `README.md`, `docs/usage.md`, `AGENTS.md`;
  - comments in `MicrovmView.qml`;
  - `docs/capture.sh`, if it relies on the full-screen layer.

## Constraints

- **The AGENTS.md sizing rule holds:** no render transform; the card derives
  from the screen and the desktop text size; the body takes the height its
  host gives.
- **Every colour is read as `Commons.Color` (#45).**
- **Keyboard focus stays exclusive while open.** No key reaches the window
  underneath.
- **The menu stays on the monitor it opens on.** It must not end up zero
  height when the target screen is unknown.
- **No new setting.** Dimming is not offered as an option.
- **Do not start before PR #47 merges.**

## Open questions

1. **How the outside click is caught without covering the screen** is a
   spec decision, to be verified live:
   - a Hyprland focus grab may close on an outside click, but may pass that
     click through;
   - if so, the fallback is a full-screen input-only layer with no scrim.

   That fallback still meets "not dimmed" and "the click does nothing else",
   but not "takes only its card's space". Is it acceptable if the focus grab
   fails?
2. **The showcase captures in `docs/img/`** show the scrim. Recommendation:
   retake them at the next capture pass, after #49 changes the form too,
   rather than in this task.
