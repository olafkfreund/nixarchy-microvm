# Intent: the menu as a card-sized layer, not a full-screen overlay

Closes #50.

## Problem

The menu (`Menu.qml`) is a `PanelWindow` anchored to all four edges of the
monitor. It is an `Overlay` layer with a scrim (`Commons.Color.menu.scrim`)
and exclusive keyboard focus. The card holding the view sits inside it,
sized from the layer: `panel.width * 0.9` and `panel.height * 0.85`.

The full-screen surface is there for one reason: "a click away closes", a
`MouseArea` over the whole layer. The cost of that one behaviour:

- **It dims the whole monitor while the menu is open.** That includes
  anything shared in a call, which is the case the owner raised on
  2026-11-16.
- **It hides everything else on that monitor.** Nothing else on the monitor
  can be seen clearly or reached while the menu is up, even though the menu
  only needs a card's worth of space.
- **Its size is tied to the screen.** The card's limits come from the
  layer, so the layer cannot shrink without the card's sizing changing too.

The layer exists only while the menu is open (`visible: root.opened`). This
is about what it does while open, not a surface left behind.

## Proposed outcome

- **The layer is the card.** The menu's surface is sized to the card and
  placed where the card is today: centred horizontally, and a third of the
  way down the focused monitor.
- **No scrim.** The rest of the monitor stays as it is.
- **A click outside still closes it, via Hyprland's focus grab**
  (`HyprlandFocusGrab`, from `Quickshell.Hyprland`, which `Menu.qml` already
  imports) instead of a full-screen `MouseArea`.
- **The card's size is derived from the screen as today.** It is the
  same share of the monitor, still from the screen and the desktop text size
  (the AGENTS.md sizing rule), now read from the target screen rather than
  from the layer.
- **Nothing else changes:**
  - the keys and look;
  - exclusive keyboard focus while open;
  - the `nixarchy-microvm-menu` namespace;
  - IPC (`toggle`, `{"create":true}`);
  - keep-loaded behaviour.

## Affected users and systems

- Everyone who opens the menu from a key or the Omarchy menu row.
- The bar popup (`Panel.qml`) is the shell's `KeyboardPanel`, not this
  layer, so it is unaffected.
- **Files:** `Menu.qml`; `docs/usage.md` if it mentions the dimming;
  `AGENTS.md` and `docs/capture.sh` if they rely on the layer covering the
  monitor.
- **Captures:** the showcase stills in `docs/img/` show the menu with its
  scrim. Whether to retake them is part of the spec.

## Constraints

- **The AGENTS.md sizing rule holds:** no render transform; the card derives
  from the screen and the desktop text size; the body takes the height its
  host gives.
- **Every colour is read as `Commons.Color` (#45).**
- **Keyboard focus stays exclusive while open.** No key may reach the window
  underneath.
- **A click outside must close the menu**, and must not reach the app under
  it as a click.
- **The layer must stay on the monitor the menu opens on.**
- **Do not start before PR #47 merges**, to avoid needless conflicts.

## Open questions

1. **Does `HyprlandFocusGrab` behave on a layer surface with exclusive
   keyboard focus?** It should clear the grab, and so close, on a click
   outside, without eating that click. The spec must verify this live. If it
   doesn't hold, there are two fallbacks:
   - keep a full-screen *input-only* layer with no scrim, so the screen is
     not dimmed but the click is still caught;
   - drop click-outside-closes, so only Esc closes.
2. **Should the screen still be dimmed a little, as a choice?** For example,
   a setting, off by default.
3. **Should the showcase captures in `docs/img/` be retaken** to show the
   new look, or left until the next capture pass?
