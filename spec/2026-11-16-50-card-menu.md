# Spec: the menu as a card-sized layer

## Design

All changes are in `Menu.qml`, plus documentation. Line numbers are on
`fix/46-review-findings` (PR #47), which this builds on.

**1. Size and place from the screen, not from the layer.**

- Add `readonly property var screenFor: root.targetScreen ||
  Quickshell.screens[0] || null`, with `screenW` and `screenH` read from it
  (0 when null).
- `viewWidth` (`:30`) uses `screenW` in place of `panel.width`.
- The card's caps become `screenW * 0.9` and `screenH * 0.85`. Today they are
  `panel.width` and `panel.height`.
- When `screenFor` is null, the card falls back to its floor width,
  `Style.space(560)`, and to its natural height. It never collapses to 0, as
  the intent requires.
- The size and offset logic (`cardWidth`, the 0.9 and 0.85 caps, and the
  one-third offset) goes into `Model.js` as `Model.menuGeometry(screenW,
  screenH, viewNatural, gaps)`, which returns `{width, height, top}`. It gets
  a Node test, including the null-screen case.

**2. The layer is the card.**

- The `PanelWindow` (`:88`) is anchored at the top only. Leaving left and
  right unanchored centres it horizontally, as layer-shell does.
- `implicitWidth` and `implicitHeight` are the card's.
- `margins.top` is the one-third offset, from `menuGeometry`.
- `exclusionMode: ExclusionMode.Ignore`, `WlrLayer.Overlay`,
  `WlrKeyboardFocus.Exclusive`, the `nixarchy-microvm-menu` namespace and
  `visible: root.opened` stay.
- The scrim `Rectangle` (`:103-106`), the full-screen `MouseArea`
  (`:109-112`) and the card's click-swallowing `MouseArea` (`:127`) are
  removed. Nothing outside the card is drawn or takes input.

**3. A click outside closes it, the way the shell's own popups do.**

- Add a `HyprlandFocusGrab` with `active: root.opened`,
  `windows: [panel]`, and `onCleared: root.close()`.
- This is the pattern the shell uses in `Ui/PopupCard.qml:87-94`: "input is
  routed only to the listed windows; clicking anywhere else clears the grab
  and we close the popup."
- `Quickshell.Hyprland` is already imported (`:3`).

**4. The fallback, used only if live verification fails.**

- If the outside click turns out to reach the app underneath, a second
  `PanelWindow` is added: full-screen, `color: "transparent"`,
  `WlrLayer.Top`, no keyboard focus. Its only content is a `MouseArea` that
  closes the menu.
- The screen is still not dimmed, and the click still does nothing else.
  This is the owner-approved fallback.
- The plan records which path was taken.

**5. Documentation.**

- **README:** the "a full-screen menu on a key" wording.
- **`docs/usage.md`:** describes the menu without the dimming.
- **AGENTS.md:**
  - `:15` and `:45` ("the full-screen keyboard menu");
  - the "Verifying live" note on `hyprctl layers -j`: the layer is now
    card-sized.
- **`MicrovmView.qml`:** the comments at `:11` and `:71`.
- **`docs/capture.sh`:** if it sizes its crop from the full-screen layer,
  it reads the card instead. Captures are not retaken in this task.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Keep the full-screen layer and drop the scrim.** The screen is no
  longer dimmed, but the menu still covers every click on the monitor. This
  survives only as the fallback in step 4.
- **Esc-only dismissal.** It drops an outcome the intent requires.
- **A dimming setting.** The intent rules it out.
- **The bar's `KeyboardPanel` for the menu as well.** It anchors to a bar
  widget, and the menu must work with no widget on the bar, as on the
  owner's host.

## Risks

- **The focus grab and an exclusive-focus Overlay layer.**
  - The shell uses the grab with popups, not with an exclusive-keyboard
    Overlay layer. Hyprland might clear the grab at once when the layer takes
    the keyboard, which would close the menu as soon as it opens.
  - Live verification covers this, and step 4 is the fallback.
- **Multi-monitor:** the layer must open on the focused monitor (`screen:
  root.targetScreen`, unchanged). It must be checked on DP-1 and on DP-3.
- **Keep-loaded:** a grab left active while the menu is closed would steal
  clicks. `active` is bound to `root.opened`.
- **Host:** only the plugin folder changes.

## Verification

- `node tests/run.js` passes with the new `menuGeometry` tests: a normal
  screen, a narrow screen hitting the cap, and a null screen.
- These pass:

  ```bash
  nix flake check
  nix flake check --all-systems --no-build
  nix build
  ```

  Then validate a fresh clone with `omarchy plugin validate`.
- Live, by AGENTS.md "Verifying live", with the owner's desktop free and an
  ai-mirror grant:
  1. Open the menu with `omarchy-shell shell toggle nixarchy.microvm '{}'`.
     `hyprctl layers -j` shows `nixarchy-microvm-menu` at card size, not
     2560x1440.
  2. A screenshot of the menu's monitor shows the rest of the screen
     undimmed.
  3. The menu does not close by itself within 3 s of opening (the risk
     above). Keys still reach it: `j`/`k` move the cursor.
  4. Click on a terminal window outside the card:
     - the menu closes;
     - the terminal does not take focus, and nothing is typed or selected
       there.

     If the terminal does react, the step 4 fallback is used and this check
     is repeated.
  5. Repeat steps 1–4 with the other monitor focused.
  6. Check the bar is healthy, and `qs log` shows no new warnings from the
     plugin.
